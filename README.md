# Bentley Family History

Research into a Bentley family line from Huddersfield, Marsden and Saddleworth, and its claimed
descent from the Bentleys of High Bentley Hall, Shelf, near Halifax, West Yorkshire.

The aim is to record every finding, with its source, so that later generations can check the
work and carry it on.

## Where things stand (10 October 2026)

The family tree on Ancestry runs from John G. Bentley of High Bentley (died 1540) to the present
day. Each generation has now been checked against records that are free to search online. In
short:

- **Well documented, 1720s–1780s, Saddleworth:** Matthew Bentley, clothier of Standedge
  (buried 1751) → James (baptised 1722) → John (baptised 1750, married Ann Broadbent 1771). The
  baptism registers name each parent, and the dates match the tree almost exactly.
- **Supported, 1850s–1990s, Huddersfield:** national index entries match Charles Bentley
  (married Sarah Dyson 1855), John D. Bentley (died 1918), Thomas Herbert Bentley (married
  **Lauretta Jones** 1920; her maiden name is a new finding) and Roy Bentley (born 1930).
- **Broken in the 1600s:** the tree passes through Jeremiah Bentley of Elland Hall, MP for
  Halifax (died 1665). The records show he was the son of George Bentley and married in 1646, so
  he can't be the father of the "John G. Bentley born 1640" who links him to Saddleworth.
- **Broken in the 1500s:** the baptism matching Richard Bentley's birth date (17 August 1554)
  names his father as Richard Bentley of Northowram, not Edward Bentley as the tree has it.
- **Halifax and Shelf in the 1500s:** Bentleys were in Shelf from at least 1296. A John
  Bentley of Shelf was buried on 1 August 1540, and his wife Agnes held land at High Bentley.
  There's no record yet linking him to Edward Bentley or to the Monmouth pedigree.

So the descent from High Bentley Hall is **not proven, and not disproven**. The documented line
currently starts with Matthew Bentley of Standedge. Whether his Saddleworth family came from the
Halifax Bentleys is now the main question. See the [research plan](research-plan.md).

## Conventions

- **Privacy.** This repository is public. Living people, and anyone who may still be living,
  are never named here. Family members can keep later generations in a private copy.
- **Evidence.** Each claim gets a status: **Documented**, **Supported**, **Recorded only** or
  **Conflicting** (defined in [family-tree.md](family-tree.md)). A family tree or pedigree,
  including Ancestry, counts as a claim to check, not as evidence.
- **Citations.** Every finding is cited down to the page or index reference, with a link to a
  scan or online record where one exists.
- **Dates.** Dates are given as written in the source. Before 1752 the year began on 25 March,
  so dates from 1 January to 24 March are written in dual form, e.g. 18 January 1578/9. GRO
  index entries give the quarter in which an event was registered, e.g. "Jan–Mar 1889".
- **Adding research.** New research goes in a dated file under `research/`. Earlier notes are
  not rewritten; `family-tree.md` and `research-plan.md` are updated instead.

- [Calderdale Companion](https://www.calderdalecompanion.co.uk/) (Malcolm Bull)
- [Historic England listing for High Bentley](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1133765)
