# Bentley Family History

Research into a Bentley family line from Huddersfield, Marsden and Saddleworth, and its claimed
descent from the Bentleys of High Bentley Hall, Shelf, near Halifax, West Yorkshire.

The aim is to record every finding, with its source, so that later generations can check the
work and carry it on.

## Where things stand (10 October 2026)

The family tree on Ancestry runs from John G. Bentley of High Bentley (died 1540) to the present
day. Each generation has now been checked against records that are free to search online, and
the two places where the tree broke have been worked through. In short:

- **The family's line is a Saddleworth one.** The records trace it back without a break to Matthew
  Bentley, clothier of Standedge (buried 1751) → James (baptised 1722) → John (baptised 1750). It
  very probably goes further: to "old Matthew" Bentley of Standedge (baptised 1664, son of Jonas
  Bentley; buried 1736), and to a Mathew Bentley with children baptised at Saddleworth in 1613.
- **Huddersfield and Marsden, 1850s–1990s:** national index entries match Charles Bentley
  (married Sarah Dyson 1855), John D. Bentley (died 1918), Thomas Herbert Bentley (married
  **Lauretta Jones** 1920) and Roy Bentley (born 1930).
- **The tree's 1600s generations come from other families.** Jeremiah Bentley was Jeremy
  Bentley of Elland Hall, MP for Halifax, son of George Bentley, a Halifax chapman. None of his
  children was a John. The "John born 1640" and "John born 1665" who link him to Saddleworth have
  no records; the John who married Grace Horsfeild was a Bingley man.
- **The tree's 1500s generations are also mixed up.** The Richard Bentley who married Isabel
  Brodley in 1576 was the son of Richard Bentley of Northowram, not of Edward Bentley of Ovenden.
  He lived at Staups House, Shibden, and died before 1600. His son John's land passed to John's
  daughters.
- **High Bentley Hall:** Bentleys were in Shelf from at least 1296. A John Bentley of Shelf was
  buried in 1540, and his widow Agnes married a Bairstow in 1542. By the 1560s the Bairstows were
  "of High Bentley".

So the descent from High Bentley Hall is **not proven**. As far as the records go, the Halifax
Bentleys and the Saddleworth Bentleys are separate families. The next step is to take the
Saddleworth line back from 1613 and see whether it leads to Halifax. See the
[research plan](research-plan.md).

## Conventions

- **Privacy.** This repository is public. Living people, and anyone who may still be living,
  are never named here. Family members can keep later generations in a private copy.
- **Evidence.** Each claim gets a status: **Documented**, **Probable**, **Supported**,
  **Recorded only** or **Conflicting** (defined in [family-tree.md](family-tree.md)). A family tree or pedigree,
  including Ancestry, counts as a claim to check, not as evidence.
- **Citations.** Every finding is cited down to the page or index reference, with a link to a
  scan or online record where one exists.
- **Dates.** Dates are given as written in the source. Before 1752 the year began on 25 March,
  so dates from 1 January to 24 March are written in dual form, e.g. 18 January 1578/9. GRO
  index entries give the quarter in which an event was registered, e.g. "Jan–Mar 1889".
- **Adding research.** New research goes in a dated file under `research/`. Earlier notes are
  not rewritten; `family-tree.md` and `research-plan.md` are updated instead.

## Main sources used so far

- *The Parish Registers of Halifax*, Vols. I–II (1538–1593), ed. E. W. Crossley:
  [baptisms](https://archive.org/details/parishregisterof37hali),
  [marriages and burials](https://archive.org/details/parishregisterof45hali)
- [*Halifax Wills*, 1389–1544](https://archive.org/details/HalifaxWillsBeingAbstractsAndTranslationsOfTheWillsRegisteredAtYorkFromTheParishOfHalifax)
- [FreeREG](https://www.freereg.org.uk/) parish register transcriptions (Saddleworth, St Chad)
- [FreeBMD](https://www.freebmd.org.uk/): the GRO birth, marriage and death indexes from 1837
- [Calderdale Companion](https://www.calderdalecompanion.co.uk/) (Malcolm Bull)
- Yorkshire Archaeological Society Record Series: indexes of wills in the York Registry
  (for example [1620–1627](https://archive.org/details/YASRS032) and
  [1636–1652](https://archive.org/details/YASRS004))
- [Historic England listing for High Bentley](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1133765)
