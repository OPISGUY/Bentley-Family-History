# Bentley Family History

The documented ancestry of a Bentley family of Huddersfield, Marsden and Saddleworth, in West
Yorkshire, traced back from the present day one generation at a time using records, cited transcriptions and explicitly labelled secondary accounts.

The aim is to record every finding with its source, so that later generations can check the
work and carry it on.

Most links in this list are supported by birth, marriage and death indexes, census returns or
parish registers. Important links remain unresolved: identifying Charles's documented father William with the William baptised in 1788; identifying the 1664 child with "old Matthew"; and establishing the younger Matthew's parentage. The relationship of Mathew (11) to Jonas is unknown, not a demonstrated father–son link. See
[family-tree.md](family-tree.md) for the chart and the evidence for each generation.

The family lived for generations at Standedge, high on the Pennines between Saddleworth and
Marsden, where they were clothiers: they made woollen cloth at home. Their farm there was known
as "Bentleys". The Saddleworth registers begin in 1613, and Mathew Bentley is the first of the
family in them. See [the Saddleworth line](research/2026-10-10-saddleworth-line.md).

**New finding, 4 November 2026:** a free SHS baptism transcription names William and Betty as Charles's parents on 8 June 1828. The father's identification with the 1788 William remains probable. See the [evidence review](research/2026-11-04-charles-william-review.md).

**Wider family populated:** [family-groups.md](family-groups.md) now organises the known households and adds 18 people across the Haigh and Platt branches and a new Bentley sibling. New findings include Sarah Ann's parents, Thomas and Harriet Haigh; Helen Bentley's Platt descendants; and further evidence for Lauretta's and Joseph's deaths. Supported identifications and unresolved conflicts are retained. [Evidence and access log](research/2026-11-04-wider-family.md).

## A note on the earlier research

The project began by testing an Ancestry family tree that traced the family to High Bentley
Hall, Shelf, near Halifax, and to a Monmouth pedigree. Checked against the records, that tree
joined several unrelated families together, so it has been set aside and the line rebuilt from
records. The earlier research is kept in `research/` because it documents the Halifax, Elland and
Shelf Bentleys, and shows why the old tree was wrong. No record so far connects this family to
High Bentley Hall.

## Conventions

- **Access.** New research uses free sources without registration. Paid copies, subscriptions and account-required routes are outside the active plan.
- **Privacy.** This repository is public. Living people, and anyone who may still be living,
  are never named here. Family members can keep later generations in a private copy.
- **Evidence.** Each claim gets a status: **Documented**, **Probable**, **Supported** or **Not
  yet proven** (defined in [family-tree.md](family-tree.md)). A family tree or pedigree,
  including Ancestry, counts as a claim to check, not as evidence.
- **Citations.** Every finding is cited down to the page, piece or index reference, with a link
  to a scan or online record where one exists.
- **Dates.** Dates are given as written in the source. Before 1752 the year began on 25 March,
  so dates from 1 January to 24 March are written in dual form, e.g. 18 January 1578/9. GRO
  index entries give the quarter in which an event was registered, e.g. "Jan–Mar 1889".
- **Adding research.** New research goes in a dated file under `research/`. Earlier notes are
  not rewritten; `family-tree.md` and `research-plan.md` are updated instead.

## Main sources used so far

- [FreeBMD](https://www.freebmd.org.uk/): the GRO birth, marriage and death indexes from 1837
- [FreeCEN](https://www.freecen.org.uk/): census returns (1851, 1861, 1871, 1891 for Marsden)
- [FreeREG](https://www.freereg.org.uk/): parish register transcriptions (Saddleworth, Halifax,
  Elland and others)
- *The Parish Registers of Halifax*, Vols. I–II (1538–1593):
  [baptisms](https://archive.org/details/parishregisterof37hali),
  [marriages and burials](https://archive.org/details/parishregisterof45hali)
- Yorkshire Archaeological Society Record Series: indexes of wills in the York Registry
- Record Society of Lancashire and Cheshire: indexes of wills proved at Chester, on
  [archive.org](https://archive.org/search?query=index+to+the+wills+and+inventories+chester)
- The National Archives' [Discovery](https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/) catalogue, for
  records held in local archives (e.g. the "Bentleys" deeds at Oldham Archives)
- [GENUKI](https://www.genuki.org.uk/): the 1379 poll tax for the West Riding
- [Calderdale Companion](https://www.calderdalecompanion.co.uk/) (Malcolm Bull)

Latest additions: [Australian households and children](research/2026-11-05-australian-households.md), with uncertain identities clearly marked; [unplaced Standedge households](research/2026-11-05-standedge-households.md) for testing possible sibling connections.
