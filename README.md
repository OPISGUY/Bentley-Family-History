# Bentley Family History

Research into the Bentley family of High Bentley Hall, Shelf, near Halifax, West Yorkshire.

The main question is whether the Bentley ancestors were established in West Yorkshire for
centuries, or whether an earlier branch came from Monmouth, Wales, as an online pedigree claims.

## Where things stand (10 October 2026)

- The online tree runs James Bentley (b. 1419) → Sir John Bentley of Monmouth (1470–1527) →
  John G. Bentley of High Bentley Hall (1504–1540), whose wife is recorded as Agnes Waterhouse. No
  record has yet confirmed any of the parent–child links.
- The Halifax parish register records the **burial of John Bentley of Shelf on 1 August 1540**,
  the same date the Ancestry tree gives for John G.'s death.
- A will dated 30 June 1540 mentions land "at the Hie Bentley" leased from **"Agnes, the late
  wif of John Bentley"**. This is the first record found that connects a John Bentley and his
  wife Agnes to High Bentley. If "late wife" means widow, though, this John was dead before the
  August burial, so there may have been two John Bentleys.
- Bentleys were in Shelf long before 1504: the family is recorded there in 1296; Thomas de Bentley
  paid poll tax there in 1379; Laurence Bentley took up land there in 1496; and Arthur Bentley was
  "of High Bentley Hall" in 1537.
- Nothing found so far supports the Monmouth link.

## Conventions

- Each claim gets a status: **Documented**, **Supported**, **Recorded only** or **Conflicting**
  (defined in [family-tree.md](family-tree.md)).
- Every finding is cited down to the page, with a link to a scan where one exists.
- Dates are given as written in the source. Before 1752 the year began on 25 March, so dates from
  1 January to 24 March are written in dual form, e.g. 14 January 1542/3.
- New research goes in a dated file under `research/`. Earlier notes are not rewritten;
  `family-tree.md` and `research-plan.md` are updated instead.
