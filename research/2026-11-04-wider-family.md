# Wider family research — 4 November 2026

This pass uses public pages without registration or payment. Transcriptions are not original register images. Earlier findings carried forward from the October notes have not all been rechecked against their original sources.

## 1. Haigh parents and the Marsden memorials

The [Marsden History Group memorial index](https://www.marsdenhistory.co.uk/downloads/graveyard_index.pdf), printed p. 7 (PDF p. 8), identifies Sarah Ann Bentley, wife of John, as a daughter of **Thomas and Harriet Haigh**, and gives her death as **10 August 1934**, aged 77. This directly records her parents; identifying her with the existing Marsden family is strongly supported by spouse, age and locality.

The adjoining John entry gives **10 November 1918**, age **67**. This conflicts with the census-based birth in 1861 and earlier death-index candidates aged 57. Preserve the discrepancy; do not alter 67 to 57. November favours the fourth-quarter candidate if this is the same man.

On pp. 26–27, Harriet, wife of Thomas, died 20 December 1866 aged 40; Thomas, husband of Harriet, died 7 November 1881 aged 60. These deaths are **candidates**, not established dates for Sarah Ann's parents.

[Collection description](https://www.marsdenhistory.co.uk/graveyard-index.php): the index is a modern transcription, not a photograph of each stone.

## 2. Helen Bentley's Platt branch

L. N. Kidd, “Some descendants of the Platt family of Thorns, Saddleworth [Part One]”, *Saddleworth Historical Society Bulletin*, 24(3), Autumn 1994, printed pp. 3–5 ([PDF](https://www.saddleworth-historical-society.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/vol24no3-2.pdf), PDF pp. 6–8), connects Helen Bentley, daughter of Matthew and Sarah, to Thomas Platt: marriage 5 January 1753. This is a secondary-source identity link, retained as **Supported**.

Kidd supplies Hannah's Shaw baptism, 4 May 1760, and the younger James's marriage to Betty Garside/Gartside, 4 January 1787. These remain secondary-source claims. His 4 February 1792 date for their son James conflicts with both SHS database versions below.

## 3. Another child of James Bentley and Mary

**Betty Bentley, baptised 4 February 1759**, parents James and Mary, Stand-edge, clothier, St Chad's. [Exact search](https://www.saddleworth-historical-society.org.uk/baptisms-search-results/?surname=Bentley&firstname=Betty&baptism-date=1759&baptism_date_variation=0); [parents search](https://www.saddleworth-historical-society.org.uk/baptisms-search-results/?surname=Bentley&fathersname=James&mothersname=Mary).

The date, parents, abode and trade support adding her between Mary (1756) and James (1761). Later same-name couples in the results are not attached.

A further date conflict affects Alice, daughter of Matthew and Sarah: the existing research gives 5 April 1741, while the [SHS Bulletin volume 12, number 1 scan](https://www.saddleworth-historical-society.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/vol12no1-2.pdf), PDF p. 15, gives 5 January 1741. The original register is needed; neither date is silently substituted.

## 4. Lauretta Bentley's death notice

The public OCR/search extract for the *Colne Valley Guardian*, **27 September 1963, p. 8**, reports Lauretta, wife of Thomas Herbert Bentley, dying **25 September**, aged 75. [Public result page](https://www.britishnewspaperarchive.co.uk/search/results/1963-09-01/1963-09-30?BasicSearch=death&MostSpecificLocation=london%2C+england&RetrieveCountryCounts=False&SomeSearch=death).

Status: **Supported, public OCR extract only**. The original newspaper image was not viewed. The named spouse makes this a strong match; September also fits the existing third-quarter death registration. The earlier family claim of July is superseded as the preferred working date, not silently erased. The OCR address is garbled and is not transcribed as fact. This notice does not identify Lauretta's parents.

## 5. Joseph in Victoria

A public [Victorian deaths 1907–1908 transcription](https://beyondthename.weebly.com/victorian-deaths-1907-1908.html) contains a 1907 entry, registration **06379**, for **Joseph Bentley**, father **William Bentley**, mother **Betty**, death place **St Kilda**, age **85**.

This supports the Australian lead for Joseph baptised in 1821: year, place, age and both parents agree. Status: **Supported**, through an unofficial index transcription; original registration not inspected. It does not verify the family's exact 3 June date, Betty's maiden name, migration date, wife or children. William's and Wright's Australian deaths remain family leads.

## 6. Candidates and exclusions

- **Betty Bottomley:** SHS includes 7 October 1781, John/Ann, Grange, Friarmere; 30 May 1782, Buckley/Betty, Greenfield; and 14 May 1780, James/Mally, Grange. [Search, page 2](https://www.saddleworth-historical-society.org.uk/baptisms-search-results/page/2/?firstname=Betty&surname=Bottomley). The census estimate cannot select one set of parents.
- **Ann Broadbent:** 26 August 1750, Elias/Anne, Green, is a candidate, not an identification of John's wife. Other Ann baptisms exist. [Search](https://www.saddleworth-historical-society.org.uk/baptisms-search-results/?firstname=Ann&surname=Broadbent&baptism-date=1750&baptism_date_variation=5).
- **Esther Bentley:** a 1755 baptism names Esther and John Kenworthey as parents of John. [Search](https://www.saddleworth-historical-society.org.uk/baptisms-search-results/?surname=Bentley&mothersname=Esther). It cannot be the Esther baptised in 1753; connection to the 1731 Esther remains unproved.
- **John Platt:** Kidd discusses competing marriages and a conflicting memorial age. No marriage or death is assigned here.
- **Mary Platt:** Kidd's apparent association of the 1754 child with a 1761 excommunication is chronologically suspect; not adopted.
- **Margaret Cartwright / Mayall, Lauretta Jones and Sarah Dyson:** searches did not establish parents. Unknowns remain unknown.
