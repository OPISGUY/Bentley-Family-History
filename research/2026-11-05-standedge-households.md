# Unplaced Bentley households around Standedge

_Checked 5 November 2026_

These baptism transcriptions document local Bentley households. **Their connection to our earlier line is not proved.** They are a research register, not additional ancestors. In particular, James and Ann must not automatically be identified with James baptised in 1784, or Matthew and Ann with an earlier Matthew.

The Saddleworth Historical Society Bentley surname results were reviewed across eleven pages. This register selects St Chad’s entries for five nearby parent pairs. It is not a complete list of children: spelling variants (including Bently), different places, other churches and missing records may yield more. Dates below are baptisms, not births. No living generations are included.

## Why keep these households separate?

Shared names, occupations and neighbouring hamlets make them useful candidates for wills and marriage searches. They do not establish a sibling relationship. A will naming a brother, an identifiable marriage, or linked baptisms and burials is needed before joining a household to the tree.

Some nonconformist entries in the same database display birth dates later than baptism dates. Those conflicting entries were not used to build these households. Even the selected St Chad’s transcriptions should eventually be compared with register images.
