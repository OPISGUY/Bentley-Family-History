# Checking the Ancestry tree against the records

10 October 2026

This file checks each generation of the Ancestry tree
([transcribed here](2026-10-10-ancestry-tree.md)) against records that are free to search
online. Every finding gives its source down to the page or index reference. Entries from the
printed Halifax registers were checked against scans of the printed pages, not just the OCR
text.

## Summary

As recorded, the tree runs from John G. Bentley of High Bentley (died 1540) to the present day.
The records support parts of it strongly, but the chain breaks in two places:

1. **Edward → Richard (1554).** The baptism that matches Richard's birth date (17 August 1554)
   names his father as *Richard* Bentley of Northowram, not Edward.
2. **The 1600s.** Jeremiah Bentley (died 1665) is Jeremy Bentley of Elland Hall, MP for Halifax.
   A local history gives his father as George Bentley, not John and Phoebe. He married in 1646
   and his known children are Abraham, Jeremy and Mary. The "John G. Bentley born 1640" and
   "John Bentley born 1665" who link him to Saddleworth don't fit: one was born before the
   marriage, and neither has a baptism record. The dates also don't work: Jeremiah would have
   been 16 and Dority 13 at the 1640 birth.

There's also no record yet linking John G. Bentley (died 1540) to Edward Bentley.

The parts that hold up:

- **1550s–1570s, Halifax:** Richard Bentley, baptised 1554, married Isabel Brodley in 1576, and a
  John son of Richard Bentley of Shibden was baptised in January 1578/9.
- **1720s–1780s, Saddleworth:** Matthew Bentley, clothier of Standedge (buried 1751) → James
  (baptised 1722) → John (baptised 1750). Every parent–child link here is named in the baptism
  register, and the dates match the tree almost exactly.
- **1850s–1990s, Huddersfield:** marriages, births and deaths in the national (GRO) indexes
  match the tree from Charles Bentley (married 1855) to Roy Bentley (born 1930).

**What this means.** As the tree stands, it does not prove that your Saddleworth Bentleys
descend from High Bentley Hall. It doesn't disprove it either. Your documented line currently
starts with Matthew Bentley of Standedge. He was probably the son of an "old Matthew" Bentley of
Standedge (buried 1736), who may be the Matthew son of Jonas Bentley baptised at Saddleworth in
1664. Whether the Saddleworth Bentleys came from the Halifax family is now the main open
question.

## Detailed findings

Abbreviations for the sources:

- **PRH I**: *The Parish Registers of Halifax*, Vol. I, Baptisms 1538–1593, ed. E. W. Crossley,
  Yorkshire Parish Register Society vol. 37 (1910).
  [Scan](https://archive.org/details/parishregisterof37hali)
- **PRH II**: *The Parish Registers of Halifax*, Vol. II, Marriages and Burials 1538–1593, ed.
  E. W. Crossley, Yorkshire Parish Register Society vol. 45 (1914).
  [Scan](https://archive.org/details/parishregisterof45hali)
- **FreeREG**: volunteer transcriptions of parish registers. The Saddleworth (St Chad) entries
  cited here come from an older transcript transcribed for FreeREG by John Lacey. Each citation
  links to the record.
- **GRO index**: the General Register Office index of births, marriages and deaths in England
  and Wales from 1837, searched through [FreeBMD](https://www.freebmd.org.uk/). References are
  district, volume and page.
- **Calderdale Companion**: Malcolm Bull's local-history encyclopaedia,
  [calderdalecompanion.co.uk](https://www.calderdalecompanion.co.uk/). A secondary source.

Halifax register entries are in Latin until the 1570s. *fil.* means son or daughter; *vx.* or
*vxor* means wife. Before 1752 the year began on 25 March, so January to March dates are given
in dual form.

### Edward Bentley of Ovenden and his wife (generation 4)

- **Burial, 23 April 1592:** "m Edward Bentley de Ovenden 23", under the heading
  "1 die Aprilis" 1592. PRH II, p. 353. This is the exact date the tree gives for Edwardus
  Bentley Sr.
- **Marriage, 1 February 1555/6:** "Edwardus Bentley & Alicia Hardi", with the day given as
  *primo*. PRH II, p. 35. The tree calls his wife **Elizabeth** Hardye. The surname matches but
  the first name doesn't. Several Edward Bentleys were living in Halifax parish at this time
  (at Northowram, Ovenden and Sowerby), so this marriage isn't certainly his.
- **Burial of his wife, 17 June 1582:** "Vx[or] Edward Bentley de Ovendē", day 17, June 1582.
  PRH II, p. 296. The tree has 21 October 1582.
- **His son Richard** was baptised 28 May 1559: "Richardus fili[us] Edwardi Bentley de Ovēden
  xxviij". PRH I, p. 120.
- **Other children of an Edward Bentley "of Ovenden"** baptised at Halifax include Elizabeth
  (Nov 1555), Sibyl (Sep 1560), Mary (Oct 1563), Grace (1566) and Samuel (1568). This list comes
  from the OCR text and hasn't been checked against the page images. No Richard born in 1554 is
  among them.
- **Link to John G. Bentley:** no record found. Edward's stated birth in 1529 predates the
  registers, so it is an estimate.

### Richard Bentley, baptised 17 August 1554: the first break (generation 5)

> Anne filia Richardi Hudson de Midgley xvij
> Richard fili[us] Richardi Bentley de North[owra]m eodem die

"Anne daughter of Richard Hudson of Midgley, the 17th. Richard son of Richard Bentley of
Northowram, the same day." August 1554. PRH I, p. 84.

This is the only baptism that matches the tree's date and place for Richard (17 August 1554,
Northowram), and it names his father as **Richard**, not Edward. So either:

- the tree's Richard is this Richard, son of Richard Bentley of Northowram, and Edward is the
  wrong father; or
- the tree's Richard is Edward of Ovenden's son (baptised 28 May 1559), and the 1554 date belongs
  to someone else. That Richard would have been 17 at the 1576 marriage below, so this seems
  less likely.

The first reading fits the other records better:

- "m Rychard Bentlaye de Northourū and his wyffe wear buryed", 20 June 1576. PRH II, p. 273.
  Richard Bentley of Northowram and his wife were buried on the same day, four months before
  the marriage below.
- Richard Bentley "of Northowram" or "of Shibden" is the father of children baptised from 1578
  onwards (see below).

### Richard Bentley and Isabel Brodley (generation 5)

- **Marriage, 22 October 1576:** "Rychard Bentlaye et Isabell Brodlye", day 22. PRH II, p. 83.
  At least two other Isabel Brodleys married at Halifax around this time: to John Marshall
  (1572, PRH II, p. 68) and to Thomas Watkinson (1577, PRH II, p. 87). So which Isabel this was
  still needs proving.
- **An infant of Richard Bentley of Northowram** was buried on 26 October 1577. PRH II, p. 278.
- **Wife of Richard Bentley of Northowram** buried 18 April 1584. PRH II, p. 302. This could be
  Isabel, but there may have been more than one Richard Bentley in Northowram. The tree's death
  for Isabel (15 Feb 1589 at Barrow-upon-Humber, Lincolnshire) has no known link to Halifax and
  is probably a different woman.
- **Richard's death** (10 June 1636, Cleckheaton) is after the printed registers end and has not
  been checked.

### Isabel Brodley's family: the Brodleys of Hipperholme

- **Isabel, daughter of Richard Brodley of Hipperholme,** baptised 26 September 1555:
  "Isabella filia Richardi Brodley de Hipom xxvj". PRH I, p. 90. The tree has "March 1555". An
  earlier Isabel, daughter of the same Richard, was baptised 11 August 1548 (PRH I, p. 54). She
  may have died young, but no burial has been looked for.
- **Elizabeth, wife of Richard Brodley of Hipperholme,** buried 5 July 1554. PRH II, p. 187. So
  the Isabel baptised in 1555 was probably the daughter of a second wife.
- **Richard Brodley of Hipperholme** buried 16 November 1557: "m Richardus Brodley de Hipom",
  day 16, with "Octobris struck out and Novembris written below in a later hand". PRH II, p. 200.
  The tree has 26 November 1557 and calls him "Major R B Bradley". There's no sign of the rank
  "Major", and military ranks of that kind weren't used in the 1550s.
- **Margaret, wife of Edward Brodley of Hipperholme,** buried 27 August 1552: "Margaret vxor
  Edwardi Brodley de Hipom xxvii". PRH II, p. 183. This is the exact date the tree gives for
  "Margaret B Lawty". It suggests Richard Brodley's father was **Edward Brodley** of
  Hipperholme. The surname Lawty is not in the record.

### John Bentley, baptised January 1578/9 (generation 6)

> Johne fil. Ric. Bentleye de Shepdē, xviij

"John son of Richard Bentley of Shibden, the 18th", January 1578/9. PRH I, p. 252.

This fits a son of Richard and Isabel (married October 1576) and the tree's "born 1579". Shibden
lies in Northowram and Southowram, and a Richard Bentley "of Northowram" had children baptised
from 1582 to 1590 (PRH I).

### The 1600s: John, Phoebe and Martha (generations 6–7)

The printed Halifax registers end in 1593, so these generations were checked against the
Calderdale Companion only:

> Bentley, John [15??–164?] Yeoman. Son of Richard Bentley. Owned Staups House, Shibden. In
> 1612, he married Phebe. Children: Martha who married Robert Bairstow, Esther who married
> Joshua Crowther, Marie who married Thomas Stone, Susannah who married Jeremy Holgate. The girls
> inherited their father's land and property.

Calderdale Companion, Bentley surname page, Ref 68-116.

This John (son of Richard, of Shibden, died in the 1640s) looks like the tree's "Jon the Elder"
(born 1579, died January 1648). But here **he** is the one who married Phebe, in 1612, and
Martha is their daughter. The tree instead gives Jon the Elder a wife Agnes Blackburne and makes
Phoebe the wife of a son John (born about 1603). Phoebe's recorded birth in 1608 can't be right
if she married in 1612. If Calderdale is right that the daughters inherited, John had no
surviving son. That is hard to reconcile with Jeremiah (below) as his grandson, or with any son
of his outliving him.

### Jeremiah Bentley of Elland Hall: the second break (generation 8)

The tree's Jeremiah Bentley (died 20 January 1665, Elland; wife Dority Dyson) is Jeremy Bentley
of Elland Hall. The Calderdale Companion has a full entry on him
([Jeremy Bentley](https://calderdalecompanion.co.uk/zz_23.html)):

- "Jeremy Bentley of Elland Hall was the son of George Bentley."
- "On 20th May 1646, he married Dorothy, daughter of Abraham Dyson, at Almondbury. Children:
  Abraham, Jeremy, Mary [b 1664] who was born posthumously."
- He was elected the first MP for Halifax on 12 July 1654, and again in 1656.
- Oliver Heywood recorded his sudden death on 17 January 1664/5 and an estate "worth well
  towards £20,000". He was buried at Elland.

The surname page adds that George Bentley "of the Old Hall – or Moot Hall, Halifax" was a
chapman with a son Jeremy (Ref 68-96).

So the records contradict the tree in three ways:

- his father was George Bentley, not John and Phoebe;
- he married in 1646, so a son "John G. Bentley born 1640" can't be from this marriage;
- John isn't among his known children.

### John G. Bentley (b. 1640) and John Bentley (b. 1665): generations 9–10

- **John G. Bentley, born 1640 at Bingley:** no record found. The tree gives no death or wife.
  His parents would have been 16 and 13 at his birth.
- **John Bentley, born 28 April 1665 at Saddleworth:** FreeREG's Saddleworth transcript has no
  John Bentley baptism in 1665. The Bentley baptisms nearest that date are Mathew, son of
  **Jonas** Bentley, on 11 December 1664
  ([record](https://www.freereg.org.uk/search_records/685e4b69865574817e6e41a7/mathew-bentley-baptism-yorkshire-west-riding-saddleworth-1664-12-11)),
  and Mary, daughter of Thomas Bentley, on 18 January 1664/5. The transcript has few Bentley
  entries between 1665 and 1690, so it may have gaps.
- The tree has this John dying at Baildon about 1737, married to Grace Horsfeild of Bingley, with
  children born at Baildon, Lightcliffe and Saddleworth. That spread suggests records from
  different families have been attached to one man.
