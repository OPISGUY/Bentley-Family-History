# Family groups

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

This is the populated companion to [the direct-line chart](family-tree.md). It includes siblings and the newly researched Haigh and Platt branches. It is not a claim that every child or spouse has been found. Living and possibly living people are excluded.

**D** = documented in the cited record, often through a transcription; **S** = supported identification or secondary-source reconstruction; **F** = family claim awaiting a record. A record naming parents documents that relationship, but matching those parents to an earlier couple can still be uncertain. Approximate census ages are not exact birth dates.

Sources used throughout:
- **R**: [October reconstruction](research/2026-10-10-line-from-records.md), with census and civil-index references. Carried forward, not all freshly rechecked.
- **SAD**: [October Saddleworth research](research/2026-10-10-saddleworth-line.md), with parish citations.
- **CW**: [William/Charles baptism review](research/2026-11-04-charles-william-review.md).
- **NEW**: [November wider-family evidence](research/2026-11-04-wider-family.md), containing exact queries, source locations and conflicts.
- **BCS**: [10 November Binn, Marsden and Carlisle Street evidence](research/2026-11-10-binn-and-carlisle-street.md), with Trove newspaper notices, Bright Cemetery entry and Marsden mill/hamlet records.

## 1. Roy Bentley and Margaret Cartwright

Roy (1930–1998) married Margaret Cartwright, registered October–December 1953, Upper Agbrigg, 2d/989. Roy's parents are Thomas Herbert Bentley and Lauretta Jones. Margaret's parents remain unidentified; Mayall is the mother's maiden name in a probable birth-index match, not a proven maternal identity. Source: R.

**Later marriages (family account):** Roy later married Betty. Margaret also remarried. Her later spouse’s name and the dates of the remarriages are unknown here. A relative reports that Margaret died before their birth and that they were six when Roy died. The Huddersfield death registered under Margaret Bentley in 1991 remains an unconfirmed candidate. Her exact birth date previously came from that same entry.

Later generations are private and deliberately absent.

The three William entries must not be collapsed into one person or assigned infant deaths without burial evidence. Harry remains a separate candidate because of the mother's apparent age and possible delayed baptism.

| Jonas Bentley of Standedge, buried 6 July 1686 | John born 16 August, baptised 27 August 1654; Mathew baptised 11 December 1664; inventory 18 August 1686 describes Jonas as **"of Standedge, Saddleworth"** (`H/KID`, p. 248) | D baptism relationships and 1686 Standedge abode, SAD, S16 |
| Mathew baptised 1664, probably “old Matthew” of Stone-edge (buried 26 February 1735/6); Ellin (buried 5 May 1719) | Ester baptised 15 September 1700; unnamed daughter buried 26 December 1702; John baptised 14 February 1702/3; **Matthew of Standedge, clothier (died 1751)** is a proposed son, supported by shared Standedge residence and four-point child-naming correlation (Ellin → Helen/Ellin, Ester & John → John & Esther, and Matthew 1738/9 born after old Matthew's 1735/6 burial); compare **Abraham Bentley, clothier**, at Acre below Deanhead in 1750 (*SHS Bulletin* 13(1), p. 15) and buried from Standedge in 1792 aged 84 | Probable identity and parent link; not a documented baptism, SAD, S16 |

Neither the **1641/2 Saddleworth Protestation Return** (Radcliffe 1887, p. 483) nor the **1664–1674 Saddleworth Hearth Tax Returns** (*SHS Bulletin* 31(3), 32(1), 33(2)) lists a Bentley—consistent with Mathewe Bentley's January 1638/9 York Castle committal order before 1641/2 and with Jonas holding at Standedge as an exempt smallholder or under-tenant in 1664–74. In the early eighteenth century, **John Bentley of Diggle, carpenter** (Admon with Inventory 18 February 1713/14, `H/KID`, p. 248; WYAS Leeds `ACC 2433`), is a chronological candidate for Jonas's 1654 son John in the hamlet immediately adjacent to Standedge, whereas **John Bentley of Boarshurst, yeoman** (executor in 1703, bondsman in 1712, joint tenant of Frenches Fulling Mill in the 1719 Manor Rental, will dated 13 August 1743 / proved 12 May 1750, and named with his mother Elizabeth on the 13 June 1750 Saddleworth Manor Court Roll) headed a separate documented branch at Boarshurst.

**The 1500s and 1400s boundary:** No Bentley appears in 15th- or 16th-century Saddleworth subsidies (1524, 1545), the 1539 Muster Roll, or the 1379 Poll Tax for Quick. East of the Pennines, a check of E. W. Crossley's *Parish Registers of Halifax* (1538–1593, Vols. 37 & 45) and Malcolm Bull's 154 *Calderdale Companion* Bentley entries (`MMB122.html`) reveals **zero occurrences of the given names *Matheus/Mathew* or *Jonas* under the surname Bentley in 16th-century Halifax/Shelf**, further separating the Saddleworth family from High Bentley Hall and Shibden. West of the Pennines, in the same Mother Parish of Rochdale, the **Bentleys of Woodhouse in Spotland** held land across the 15th, 16th and 17th centuries (*VCH Lancaster* Vol. 5; wills of Jane Bentley 1639 and Arthur Bentley 1650 in *RSLC* Vol. 4), using the forenames William and Arthur. No descent from High Bentley Hall is asserted, and no primary record yet names the father of Mathew (fl. 1613–14) or Jonas (d. 1686). [Full 11 November 1400s–1800s analysis](research/2026-11-11-standedge-to-1600s-and-beyond.md).
