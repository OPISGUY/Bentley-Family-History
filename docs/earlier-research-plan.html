  • ✓ Locate a Mathew Bentley wounded in the 1619–20 Manchester constables’ accounts and a Mathewe Bentley committal order in January 1638/9. Keep both unlinked to the pedigree.
  • ○ Test those two men against the 1613 and 1614 parish household. Do not infer a migration motive from the legal records.
  • ✓ Locate SHS catalogue H/KID, p. 248, listing copies of Jonas’s inventory (18 August 1686, explicitly describing him as “of Standedge, Saddleworth”) and Matthew’s will (18 November 1751, proved 3 March 1753).
  • ✓ Check the 1641/2 Saddleworth Protestation Return (Radcliffe 1887, p. 483) and 1664–1674 Saddleworth Hearth Tax Returns (SHS Bulletin 31(3), 32(1), 33(2)): no Bentley listed, consistent with Mathewe Bentley's January 1638/9 York Castle committal order and Jonas holding at Standedge as an exempt smallholder or under-tenant.
