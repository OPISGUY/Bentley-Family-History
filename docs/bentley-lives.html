Twenty Chapters Across Four Centuries · 1613–1998

The Lives of the Bentleys

Commercial genealogy sites turn people into isolated dates on a chart. Here, twenty chronological chapters follow the Bentleys from a May 1613 christening at St Chad’s, Saddleworth and a 1686 inventory on the high ridge of Standedge to the woollen mills and stone quarries of Marsden and three brothers’ houses on Carlisle Street in St Kilda.
