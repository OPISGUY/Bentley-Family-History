Source checks and new findings

10 October 2026

2. Burial of John Bentley of Shelf, 1 August 1540

The Halifax parish register records this burial:

> Incip<sup>t</sup> hic mes̃ August. An<sup>o</sup> Dñi 1540 > Joh̃es Bentley de Schelff p<sup>r</sup>mo die

Translation: "Here begins the month of August, AD 1540. John Bentley of Shelf, [buried] the first day."

  • Source: The Parish Registers of Halifax, Vol. II: Marriages and Burials, 1538 to 1593,

    • transcribed and indexed by E. W. Crossley, Yorkshire Parish Register Society vol. 45 (issued for 1912, printed 1914), p. 138. Scan: <https://archive.org/details/parishregisterof45hali>

  • Checked against the page image: yes.
  • What it shows: a John Bentley of Shelf was buried at Halifax on 1 August 1540. This is the

    • same date as John G. Bentley's death in the Ancestry tree, which suggests the Ancestry date comes from this entry, or from an index of it. Strictly, it is a burial date, not a death date.

  • What it doesn't show: his age, parents, wife, or any link to High Bentley. He is not

    • described as anyone's son (the register uses "fil." for children), so he was probably an adult.

  • The same page records the burial of William Brooke of Hipperholme on 13 July 1540 (see

    • section 3).

Halifax's registers begin in November 1538 (burials from 22 November 1538). There is therefore no baptism record for a man born in 1504, so his recorded 1504 birth date has to be an estimate.

3. Agnes, "the late wife of John Bentley", and land at High Bentley (1540)

The will of William Brooke of Hipperholme, dated 30 June 1540, ends:

> Forthermore I assigne unto my saide wif and childer one close lienge at the Hie Bentley, called > the Rondeynge, whiche I haue taken of Agnes, the late wif of John Bentley, to me and my > assignes for eght yeres next insuynge the date herof.

That is: Brooke leaves to his wife and children a field at High Bentley called the Rondeynge, which he had leased from Agnes, the late wife of John Bentley, for eight years.

  • Source: *Halifax Wills: being abstracts and translations of the wills registered at York

    • from the parish of Halifax*, Part II (1515–1544), ed. E. W. Crossley, will no. 244, pp. 133–134. The printed reference to the original is "Reg. Test. xi. 427": York probate register vol. 11, folio 427. The will was proved on 3 August 1540. Scan: <https://archive.org/details/HalifaxWillsBeingAbstractsAndTranslationsOfTheWillsRegisteredAtYorkFromTheParishOfHalifax>

  • Checked against the page image: yes.
  • Why it matters: this is the first contemporary record found that names a John Bentley and a

    • wife called Agnes, and connects them to land at High Bentley. It supports the family tree's "John Bentley of High Bentley, wife Agnes". It does not give Agnes's maiden name.

The conflict

In wills of this period, "the late wife of John Bentley" usually means John Bentley's widow. If it does here, John was already dead by 30 June 1540. But the register shows a John Bentley of Shelf buried on 1 August 1540, a month later. Possible explanations:

1. Two John Bentleys. Agnes's husband died earlier, before the register begins in November 1538 or with his burial recorded somewhere else. The man buried on 1 August 1540 was someone else, perhaps a son. A text search of the register found no other John Bentley burial between November 1538 and July 1540. 2. Agnes had died, not John. "Late wife" can also mean a wife who had died. Then John could be the man buried in August 1540. The difficulty is that a married woman could not normally lease out land in her own name, which suggests Agnes was a widow when she made the lease. 3. An error in a date. Both printed entries match their page images, so this would have to be an error in the original registers or in the 1900s transcriptions. Only the originals can settle it.

Until this is resolved, John G. Bentley in the Ancestry tree may be two men combined: a John who held High Bentley and left a widow Agnes, and a John of Shelf buried on 1 August 1540.

4. Agnes Bentley, widow, remarried 20 November 1542

> Edwardus Berstowe et Agnes Bentley vidua … vicesimo die [mensis Novembris]

  • Source: Crossley, Parish Registers of Halifax, Vol. II, p. 11 (marriages 1542–1543). The

    • month heading ("quinto die mes Noveb.") is at the foot of p. 10. Checked against the page images. (The OCR text wrongly places this entry in January.)

  • Why it matters: an Agnes Bentley who was a widow married Edward Berstowe (Bairstow) at

    • Halifax. She could be John Bentley's widow from section 3, but Agnes was a common name. At least one other Agnes Bentley, not described as a widow, married Edward Wood at Halifax in 1540 (OCR text; not checked against the page image).

Arthur Bentley's 1537 entry matters most: it puts a different Bentley "of High Bentley Hall" in the middle of John G.'s recorded lifetime. Arthur may have been John's father, brother or kinsman, or the tree may have the wrong man as holder of the hall.

Later history of High Bentley Hall

From the Calderdale Companion entry for High Bentley Hall (Ref H79), which lists four books on the house: Aisled Houses in the Halifax Area, Ancient Halls in & about Halifax, Halifax Antiquarian Society Transactions and The Old Halls & Manor Houses of Yorkshire:

  • "It was the home of the Benteley family, including Arthur Bentley [1537]."
  • Later held by the Wade family, yeoman clothiers. The inscriptions "RW 1661 RW IW 1661" on a

    • door lintel and over the hall fireplace are for Richard Wade and his wife Jane. The stone casing of 1661 dates from their time.

  • In the later 17th century it was occupied by Joseph Northend and passed down his family.
