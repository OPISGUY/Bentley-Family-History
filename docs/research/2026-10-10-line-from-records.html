The line rebuilt from records

10 October 2026

The Ancestry tree has been set aside. This line starts with Roy Bentley (1930–1998), whom the family knows to be the son of Thomas Bentley. Each earlier generation has been added only where a record supports it. Every source is cited below.

Roy's descendants are not included in this public record.

So the records carry the line back without a gap to William and Betty Bentley, married at Saddleworth in 1815. One record is missing to connect William to the Saddleworth Bentleys of the 1700s, and through them back to 1613: his baptism, which the family tree gives as 8 April 1788, son of John and Ann. Until it is found, the line is traced on the working assumption that William was their son.

Generation by generation

1. Roy Bentley (1930–1998) and Margaret Cartwright

  • Birth: Roy Bentley, registered Apr–Jun 1930, Huddersfield, vol. 9a, p. 436, **mother's

    • maiden name Jones** (GRO index). This links him to Thomas H. Bentley and Lauretta Jones.

  • Marriage: Roy Bentley and Margaret Cartwright, Oct–Dec 1953, Upper Agbrigg district,

    • vol. 2d, p. 989. Each entry gives the other's surname (GRO index).

  • Death: 1998, Fartown, Huddersfield (family knowledge; not yet checked).
  • Updated from family testimony: Both Roy and Margaret married again. Roy’s later wife was Betty. Margaret died before the reporting relative was born, and the relative was six when Roy died.
  • Unresolved index match: The 1991 Huddersfield death entry for Margaret Bentley, register 4, entry 1629, gives a birth date of 2 June 1934. No record connects that entry to Roy’s first wife after her remarriage. The exact birth and death dates from it are unconfirmed.
  • Possible birth: Margaret Cartwright, Huddersfield, Apr–Jun 1934, 9a/432, mother Mayall. Her marriage to Roy is documented, but this birth identification needs independent support.

6. William Bentley's parents: John Bentley and Ann Broadbent (probable)

> Update, 10 October 2026: William's baptism has since been found on FreeREG, under the > spelling "Bently": 8 April 1788, William, son of John and Ann Bently, of Meerleech, clothier. > His parents are now documented. See the Saddleworth line.

The Ancestry tree records William as William Standage Bentley, born 8 April 1788 at "Meerlech", Saddleworth, son of John Bentley (1750–1789). It also gives him a marriage on 23 Dec 1814 at St Chad (probably the banns, ahead of the 5 January 1815 wedding) and a death on 16 April 1835 at Ridge, Saddleworth, aged 47. The records behind these facts haven't been seen yet: the 1788 baptism isn't in the FreeREG transcript.

What the Saddleworth registers show about John and Ann. John Bentley and Ann Broadbent married at Saddleworth on 2 April 1771. Their children's baptisms and burials, all naming both parents (FreeREG, St Chad, Saddleworth):

Why William is probably their son:

  • Place. John and Ann lived at Standedge (also written Stone edge and "Stannage"). William

    • and Betty's sons were born at "Stanage" (Joseph, 1821) and Standedge (Wright, 1831), according to the Ancestry tree. "Standage" in William's name on Ancestry is very probably that place name, taken from a register entry, rather than a real middle name.

  • The name. John and Ann's first William was baptised and buried in July 1779. Reusing a dead

    • child's name for the next son was normal, so a second William in 1788 fits.

  • Dates. William's ages at marriage (26) and death (47) fit a birth in 1788.
  • Naming. William and Betty's eldest son was John, after William's father, as the naming

    • custom of the time would lead you to expect.

What still needs checking:

  • At least four John Bentleys were having children baptised at Saddleworth in the 1770s and

    • 1780s: with Ann at Standedge, with Mary at Boarshurst, with Sarah (Sally) at Delph, and with Jane. So the 1788 baptism must name Ann as the mother, or give the abode Standedge, to prove the link. A record naming only "John" would not be enough.

  • The Ancestry tree's "brother William Bentley (1781–1805)" doesn't fit the registers: the 1781

    • baby was John, and the William of 1779 died at two days old. That person is probably attached to the wrong parents.

If William is John and Ann's son, the line continues back through documented Saddleworth generations to Matthew Bentley of Standedge (died 1751), and very probably to a Mathew Bentley in 1613 (see the two breaks).

How to close the gap

> Update, 10 October 2026: step 1 is done (see the update in section 6). Steps 2–5 would > confirm that the William baptised in 1788 is Charles's father.

1. William's baptism, 8 April 1788: open the record attached to the Birth fact on Ancestry (or search FamilySearch, which is free with an account). It should name the mother and the abode. "Ann" or "Standedge" would settle it. 2. Charles's baptism, 1 May 1828: open the record attached to Charles's Birth fact. It should name William and Betty. 3. Charles's marriage certificate, Jul–Sep 1855, Huddersfield, vol. 9a, p. 262. It gives Charles's father's name and occupation. It can be ordered from the General Register Office. 4. William's burial, 1835, and Betty's, 1859: Saddleworth or Marsden burial registers. 5. The 1841 census (not on FreeCEN for this area): Betty, probably at Saddleworth or Marsden, with the younger children.
