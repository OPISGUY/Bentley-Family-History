Charles Bentley's parents: free-source review

4 November 2026

Finding

The Saddleworth Historical Society (SHS) public baptism transcription records Charles Bentley, baptised 8 June 1828 at St Chad's, child of William and Betty, abode Standage, occupation clothier. This is direct documentary evidence, through a transcription, of the named parents. The original register image has not been inspected; the result does not display a register page or entry number.

Charles search, accessed 4 November 2026. The date displayed is 8-6-1828.

The earlier notes treated 1 May 1828 as a possible baptism, but elsewhere described it as a birth date from the Ancestry tree. Keep that birth claim unverified; use 8 June 1828 for the baptism. The new result neither confirms nor disproves a birth on 1 May.

These are baptism dates, not birth dates. The repeated Williams may represent children who died and whose name was reused, but burials have not been found to establish that. Harry is a candidate additional sibling; Betty's age in the previously recorded 1851 census makes a late birth noteworthy, and the transcription gives no birth date. A delayed baptism, a transcription error or another couple must be considered. Do not automatically add every result as a confirmed sibling.

The Charles search also returned a Charles baptised 6 June 1819, parents James and Ann, Standage, clothier, and a 1796 Charles at Friarmere. The 1828 entry fits the target's age and mother in the 1851 census much better than the 1819 entry. Shared abode and trade alone do not identify a person.

What is established, and what remains uncertain?

1. Charles's named parents: William and Betty are documented in the 1828 baptism transcription. Identification with the Charles in the previously cited 1851 household is strongly supported by age, locality, mother's name and the John/William/Wright family pattern. 2. Betty's maiden name: identification with Betty Bottomley in the previously cited 5 January 1815 marriage remains supported by chronology and locality; the baptism itself does not give her maiden name. 3. William's identity: this does not prove that Charles's father was the William baptised 8 April 1788 to John and Ann Bently. That remains probable. The chart retains a dashed connection at this identity step. 4. William's death: if Harry belongs to this household, the status “Widow” supports Betty being widowed by 29 November 1835 and the Ridge locality. It does not establish the claimed 16 April 1835 death/burial or age 47. Death and burial dates must not be silently interchanged. 5. Alternative candidate: William, baptised 4 April 1779 to Thomas Rhodes and Esther Bentley, remains unresolved. The earlier “only likely candidate” wording is too strong without excluding him and possible migrants.

Citation audit and search log

  • Read the existing README, tree, plan and the two 10 October research notes. These supply the earlier census, marriage and 1788 baptism citations; their content is prior research, not independently reverified evidence from this session.
  • Attempted the cited 1788 FreeREG baptism, 1815 FreeREG marriage and 1851 FreeCEN census. Web retrieval failed; direct anonymous requests returned HTTP 503. This is an access failure, not a negative genealogical result.
  • Found SHS through searches for Saddleworth registers, then used its actual public search form. Its archives guide lists St Chad's baptisms for 1801–1837, with more to add. Retrieved the Charles and William/Betty results without signing in or paying.
  • Web searches included "Charles Bentley" "1828" Saddleworth, "William Bentley" "16 April 1835", "Bentley" "Ridge" "1835", "Charles Bentley" "Sarah Dyson" 1855 and "William Bentley" "Saddleworth" "burial" "47". No inspected result supplied the target burial or full 1855 marriage entry. This was not an exhaustive register search.
  • No original 1828 baptism image, 1835 burial entry or 1855 marriage entry was obtained. The baptism transcription is the substantive new evidence.
