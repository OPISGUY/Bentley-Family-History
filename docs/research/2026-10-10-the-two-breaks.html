The two breaks in the tree

10 October 2026 (follow-up to the tree check)

The tree check found two places where the Ancestry tree breaks:

1. Edward → Richard (1554): was the Richard who married Isabel Brodley the son of Edward Bentley of Ovenden, as the tree says, or of Richard Bentley of Northowram? 2. The 1600s: how does Jeremiah Bentley (died 1665) connect to John Bentley above him and to the Saddleworth Bentleys below him?

This file settles both as far as online records allow, using the Halifax, Elland, Bingley, Birstall and Saddleworth registers on FreeREG; the printed Halifax registers (to 1593); the published indexes of York wills; and the Calderdale Companion. Sources are listed at the end.

The answers

Break 1. The Richard who married Isabel Brodley was almost certainly the son of Richard Bentley of Northowram, not Edward of Ovenden. He lived at Staups House, Shibden, and died before 1600. His son John Bentley of Staups House left his land to his daughters.

Break 2. Jeremiah is Jeremy Bentley of Elland Hall, son of George Bentley, a Halifax chapman. He has nothing to do with John and Phoebe. His children are all in the Elland register from 1647 on, and none is a John. The "John born 1640" and "John born 1665" who carry the tree on to Saddleworth have no records. The John Bentley who married Grace Horsfeild was married at Bingley in 1684.

What this means. The Ancestry tree joins two separate families:

  • The Staups House Bentleys of Shibden, Halifax. Their male line ends with John Bentley of

    • Staups House (died 1620s–1640s), whose daughters inherited.

  • The Saddleworth Bentleys. The records trace them back without a break to Matthew Bentley

    • of Standedge (died 1751), and very probably to Jonas Bentley in the 1650s and a Mathew Bentley in 1613, when the Saddleworth registers begin.

So the correct line to take further back is the Saddleworth one. No record yet connects it to Halifax or High Bentley.

Break 1: which Richard?

The Richard Bentleys of Halifax parish, 1550–1621

The registers show several Richard Bentleys at the same time. Three matter here.

Richard Bentley of Northowram (the elder)

  • His son Richard was baptised on 17 August 1554: "Richard fili[us] Richardi Bentley de

    • North[owra]m eodem die" (PRH I, p. 84). This is the exact date the tree gives for its Richard.

  • "Rychard Bentlaye de Northourū and his wyffe wear buryed", 20 June 1576 (PRH II, p. 273).
  • No will is indexed for him at York, so he probably died intestate.

Richard Bentley of Shibden and Staups House (the younger)

  • Married Isabel Brodley on 22 October 1576, four months after his father's burial: "Rychard

    • Bentlaye et Isabell Brodlye" (PRH II, p. 83).

  • An infant of "Ric. Bentley de Northour." was buried on 26 October 1577 (PRH II, p. 278).
  • His son John was baptised on 18 January 1578/9: "Johne fil. Ric. Bentleye de Shepdē" (PRH I,

    • p. 252).

  • The Calderdale Companion describes him as "Of Staups House, Shibden. He married Isabel.

    • Child: John" (Ref 68-87). Of Isabel it says: "After Richard's death, she married Edward Sawley" (Ref 68-93).

  • Isabel's remarriage: "Edw: Sawley" of Northowram married "Isabel Bentleye" at Halifax on

    • 18 August 1600 (FreeREG). So Richard was dead by August 1600. No burial for him appears in the printed registers to 1593, so he probably died between 1593 and 1600. The Halifax burials for those years aren't transcribed online.

Richard Bentley of Ovenden, son of Edward

  • Baptised 28 May 1559, "Richardus fili[us] Edwardi Bentley de Ovēden" (PRH I, p. 120).
  • A Richard Bentley of Ovenden had children baptised at Halifax in 1580, 1582, 1585/6 and 1589

    • (PRH I, OCR text), and again in 1595 and July 1599 (1595, 1599).

Why the tree's Richard is the Northowram one

  • The date. The tree's birth date for Richard, 17 August 1554 at Northowram, belongs to the

    • son of Richard Bentley of Northowram.

  • The timing. The elder Richard of Northowram was buried in June 1576. His son married four

    • months later, as a young man coming into his inheritance often did.

  • The place. Isabel's husband and their son John lived at Shibden, in Northowram township.

    • Edward's son Richard stayed at Ovenden, a few miles away, raising his own family there through the 1580s and 1590s.

  • The deaths. Isabel's husband was dead by August 1600. The Ovenden Richard was still having

    • children baptised in July 1599 (the same man, or his son of the same name).

This isn't absolute proof: no single document says "Richard son of Richard married Isabel". That would most likely come from the Wakefield manor court rolls, which record heirs taking up copyhold land. Shibden and Northowram were in the manor of Wakefield. But every record points the same way, and none supports Edward.

Corrections to the tree:

  • Richard did not die on 10 June 1636 at Cleckheaton. He was dead by 1600, and no Richard

    • Bentley burial for 1636 appears in the Birstall (Cleckheaton) register.

  • Isabel did not die in 1589 at Barrow-upon-Humber. She remarried at Halifax in 1600.
  • The burial of "Vx[or] Ric. Bentley de Northourū" on 18 April 1584, mentioned in the tree check

    • as possibly Isabel, must be the wife of a different Richard.

  • The will of "Richard Bentley, Northowram, clothier", dated 7 July 1640 (YAS RS 4), belongs to a

    • later Richard.

John Bentley of Staups House and his daughters

  • Baptised 18 January 1578/9, son of Richard Bentley of Shibden (above).
  • Probably married Agnes Blackburne at Halifax on 24 September 1599

    • (FreeREG). This is the couple the tree calls "Jon Bentley the Elder" and Agnes Blackburne. Several John Bentleys married at Halifax around then, but the children below fit.

  • Daughters baptised at Halifax, father "John Bentley of Northowram":

    • Susan, 26 Jan 1605, Martha, 24 Apr 1608 and Esther, 10 Mar 1610. The transcript gives these dates without dual years.

  • The Calderdale Companion (Ref 68-116): "Yeoman. Son of Richard Bentley. Owned Staups House,

    • Shibden. In 1612, he married Phebe. Children: Martha who married Robert Bairstow, Esther who married Joshua Crowther, Marie who married Thomas Stone, Susannah who married Jeremy Holgate. The girls inherited their father's land and property." Since Susan, Martha and Esther were born before 1612, they were Agnes's children and Phebe was John's second wife.

  • Staups House: "House mentioned as the home of John Bentley in 1607. It is dated IC 1684 for

    • his grandson, John Crowther" (Calderdale Companion, Ref S193). So the house passed down through the daughters.

  • Phoebe Longbotham: the only Phebe Longbotham baptism found is on 29 October 1609, daughter

    • of Samuel Longbotham of Warley (FreeREG). That's probably the source of the tree's "born 1608", but a girl born in 1609 can't be the Phebe who married John in 1612. Phebe's maiden name is unknown.

  • The tree's second John (born about 1603, married Phoebe) has no baptism. The Halifax

    • transcript has a gap from 1601 to 1605, so that alone proves little. But he seems to be the same man as John the Elder, split in two.

  • When did John die? A "John Bentley, Northowram, yeoman" made his will on 18 August 1626,

    • proved 13 July 1627 (York Reg. 39, f. 214; YAS RS 32). The Calderdale Companion gives "[15??–164?]" and the tree gives 21 January 1648. Reading the 1626 will would settle whether it is his.

Break 2: Jeremiah Bentley and the two Johns

Jeremy Bentley of Elland Hall

  • His father. The Calderdale Companion gives his father as George Bentley "of the Old Hall –

    • or Moot Hall, Halifax. He was a chapman. … Child: Jeremy" (Ref 68-96; foldout ZZ_23). The York index lists the will of "Bentley, George, Hallifax, chapman", dated 19 July 1623 and proved 18 September 1624 (York Reg. 38, f. 230; YAS RS 32). That will should name George's children.

  • George's family. A George Bentley married Mary Waterhouse at Halifax on 17 January 1596/7

    • (FreeREG). A George Bentley of Halifax had children baptised in 1610 (Grace), 1617 (Timothy), 1619 (Elizabeth) and 1621 (Anna). A Timothy Bentley was among the 59 voters when Jeremy was elected in 1654. No baptism for Jeremy has been found: the Halifax transcript has no Bentley baptisms from 1622 to 1645. The tree's "1624, Almondbury" is unconfirmed. Almondbury is where he married.

  • His marriage. He married Dorothy, daughter of Abraham Dyson, at Almondbury on 20 May 1646

    • (Calderdale Companion).

  • His children, all baptised at Elland (FreeREG, Elland parish register):

This matches the Calderdale Companion ("Abraham, Jeremy, Mary [b 1664] who was born posthumously"). There is no John, and no child before the 1646 marriage. Naming a son George fits a father called George.

  • His death: 17 January 1664/5, from Oliver Heywood's diary. His burial at Elland hasn't been

    • found in the transcript.

The tree's John (born 1640) and John (born 1665)

  • No baptism for either. FreeREG's West Riding transcripts list fifteen John Bentley baptisms

    • between 1636 and 1670: at Elland, Bolton-by-Bowland, Methley, Bradford, Calverley, Halifax, Bingley, Saddleworth, Leeds and Fewston. None is in 1640 or on 28 April 1665.

  • John Bentley married Grace Horsfeild at Bingley on 8 February 1684 (as transcribed)

    • (FreeREG). So the tree's John (born 1665, married Grace, died at Baildon about 1737) was a Bingley and Baildon man. Attaching him to Saddleworth, and making him Matthew's father, has no support.

How these fit together:

  • Mathew, son of Jonas (baptised 1664) is the only Mathew baptism in the right period. He

    • would have been 71 in 1736, which fits "old Matthew". A Mathew was having children in 1700–1703, and his wife Ellin died in 1719.

  • Matthew of Standedge (died 1751, recorded as born about 1692) lived and was buried at the same

    • farm, Stone-edge (Standedge). An "old Matthew" there implies a younger Matthew at the same place. That makes old Matthew very probably his father. Matthew's own baptism hasn't been found: the transcript has only two Bentley baptisms between 1690 and 1700, so there is probably a gap.

  • Jonas Bentley had sons in 1654 and 1664. "Jonah Bentley" buried in 1686 is probably him.
  • The earliest Saddleworth Bentley found is a Mathew with children baptised in 1613 and 1614.

    • He may be the "Mathey" buried in 1657/8. How he relates to Jonas is not yet known. The Saddleworth registers start in 1613 (bishop's transcripts from 1612).

  • A lead: Bentley Farmhouse, Whimberry Lee Lane, Bleak Hey Nook, Saddleworth, is a mid-18th-

    • century Grade II listed farmhouse (list entry 1068123) near Standedge. It was probably named after this family.

Probable line: Mathew (fl. 1613) → ? → Jonas (fl. 1654–1664) → Mathew (bap. 1664, "old Matthew", d. 1736) → Matthew of Standedge (d. 1751) → James (bap. 1722) → John (bap. 1750) → …

A lead on High Bentley Hall

These records suggest how High Bentley passed out of the Bentley name:

  • 30 June 1540: "Agnes, the late wife of John Bentley" held land at High Bentley

    • (source checks).

  • 20 November 1542: Agnes Bentley, widow, married Edward Berstowe (Bairstow) (PRH II,

    • p. 11).

  • 17 January 1562/3: "Joh̃es Berstowe de Hie Bentlei" buried (PRH II, p. 221).
  • 12 January 1576: will of Thomas Bairstowe of High Bentley in Shelf (YAS RS 19).
  • November 1577: John, son of John Bayrstall of High Bentley, buried (PRH II, p. 278).
  • 1661 onwards: the Wade family. The house was cased in stone in 1661 for Richard Wade, and a

    • will of Richard Waide of High Bentley is dated 2 May 1666 (YAS RS 60).

If Agnes's interest in High Bentley passed to the Bairstows through her second marriage, that would explain why no Bentleys are found at High Bentley after the 1540s. It also points the search for the High Bentley Bentleys' own heirs to John Bentley of Shelf's children, who aren't yet identified.
