Beyond names and dates

The lives behind the tree

The records leave us stories, but also questions. Here is what we can reasonably say so far.

The earliest generations

Was the first Bentley running from something?

We do not know who first arrived or why. Two new legal-record leads name a Mathew Bentley in 1620 and a Mathewe in 1638/9. Neither is yet identified as an ancestor.

Read the records, without the guesswork →
A place to make a living

Why settle among the hills?

Later Bentleys were clothiers. Hillside farms and packhorse routes supported a working textile economy. That gives us context, but not the earliest family’s personal motive.
