Matthew and Sarah Bentley · Saddleworth, 1722–51

The clothier at Knoll

When James was baptised in March 1722, his parents, Matthew and Sarah, lived at Dean-head. Their daughter Mary’s entry gives Knowll. By the time another daughter, Sarah, was baptised, the place written beside her name was Stone-edge. A whole childhood could be spent within a small patch of these hills and still leave a string of different addresses.

Matthew was a clothier. Cloth made in Saddleworth could travel over the hills by packhorse to Huddersfield. The Pennines look rather less like the edge of the world when you picture a load of woollen cloth going to market.

At home the years brought births and losses. In April 1736 Matthew and Sarah had their daughter Anna baptised. That July they buried their young son John. Another Matthew followed, then Alice, then Betty. More than twenty years separated the baptisms of their eldest James and youngest Betty. When their father was buried in November 1751, the two details the clerk chose for him were his home and his trade. He was Matthew Bentley of Stone-edge, clothier.
