James Bentley and Mary Nield · Saddleworth, 1745–61

The day after Christmas

On the day after Christmas in 1745, James Bentley and Mary Nield married in Saddleworth. He was the son of Matthew and Sarah, baptised twenty-three years earlier. When the couple began a family, the address alongside their children’s names was simply Barn.

Sarah came first in the surviving baptisms, in November 1748. John followed two years later. By Esther’s baptism in 1753 the family were giving their home as Stone-edge, and it was there that Mary, Betty and another James joined them.

Father and son shared more than a surname. The word written for James’s trade was the same one used for Matthew’s: clothier. And the boy John, baptised while his parents were at Barn, would one day earn that same description himself. Three working lives are tucked into that one word. Mary’s own work went unrecorded, though her name runs through the years from the Christmas wedding to the baptism of their youngest son.
