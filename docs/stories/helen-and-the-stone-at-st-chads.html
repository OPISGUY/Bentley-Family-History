Helen Bentley and Thomas Platt · Saddleworth, 1752–89

Eight days at Heyhouse

On 24 August 1769, a baby from Heyhouse was baptised Joseph. His parents were Thomas Platt and Helen Bentley. Eight days later Helen died. She was in her thirty-sixth year.

Helen had first appeared as a mother nearly seventeen years earlier. Her eldest girl, Sarah, was baptised in December 1752, when Helen was living at Stand-edge and Thomas, a clothier, was at Lee. The following January they married. Over the years their children’s names turn up at Stone-edge, Denshaw and Heyhouse. Different clerks wrote Helen, Ellen and Ellin. They were writing the name of the same mother.

Helen was buried on 9 September. In November the family buried little Joseph too. Thomas lived another twenty years, dying in 1789. In the old yard at Saddleworth Church a stone commemorated them together, with Thomas remembered as being of Whams Inn. For anyone visiting the churchyard, that is the place to picture them together. For anyone reading their story, it is worth lingering over the eight days in which Helen and her youngest boy were both alive.
