# Friday’s paper

Thomas Herbert Bentley and Lauretta Jones · Marsden and Huddersfield, 1888–1965

On Friday 27 September 1963 the Colne Valley Guardian carried word of Lauretta Bentley’s death. She had died on Wednesday, aged seventy-five. The notice named her husband, Thomas Herbert. Forty-three years after their wedding, the two names were still appearing together in print.

Thomas had been the two-year-old in the weaver’s house at Binn, with an older brother Willie, an older sister Sarah and a little brother Sam. Lauretta had been registered as Lauretta Jones in the Halifax district. In the first months of 1920 their marriage was registered at Huddersfield. Dorothy and Roy were the children remembered by the family.

Thomas outlived Lauretta by about a year and a half. His death was registered in the spring of 1965, aged seventy-seven. Across those years there must have been any number of ordinary days nobody thought to write down. The local paper kept one that mattered: the Wednesday in September when Lauretta died, and people who knew her would have wanted to know.
