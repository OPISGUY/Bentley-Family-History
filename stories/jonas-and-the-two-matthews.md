# Eleven days in August

Jonas, John and Mathew Bentley · Saddleworth, 1654–1736

John Bentley was eleven days old when he was baptised. He had been born on 16 August 1654, a detail someone thought worth preserving alongside the date of his baptism on the twenty-seventh. His father was Jonas. Ten winters later Jonas had another son baptised, a December boy called Mathew.

By July 1686 Jonas himself was gone. A burial entry calls him Jonah, and an inventory of his goods was made that summer. His sons, if both were still living, would then have been about thirty-one and twenty-one.

The younger boy carried the same name as a Bentley father recorded in Saddleworth half a century before him. Whether Jonas belonged to that earlier household is still an open question. In his own family, though, he has a clear place. He is the father who appears twice, once beside an August baby and once beside a December one.
