# A name given again

John Bentley and Ann Broadbent · Standedge, 1771–89

In August 1776 John and Ann gave a son his father’s name. He was baptised on the twenty-first and buried on the twenty-third. The following August they called another son John. Again, just two days lay between baptism and burial. When a little William was baptised in July 1779, his burial followed two days later too.

In 1781 another child of theirs was baptised John. In 1788 there was another William. The repetition can be bewildering to a descendant trying to draw the tree. For John and Ann, each time the name belonged to a different child. Each deserves a place of his own.

John earned his living as a clothier at Standedge. He had married Ann in 1771. In November 1789 he too was buried, aged about thirty-nine. Their youngest William had been baptised only nineteen months before. Of all the questions asked about that boy in later years, one thing about his beginnings is plain. His father had known him for only a very small part of a lifetime.
